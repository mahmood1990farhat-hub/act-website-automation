# ACT driver-application policy review

**REVIEW DRAFT v0.2 | 21 October 2026 | NOT EFFECTIVE OR APPROVED FOR PUBLICATION**

Prepared for Airport & City Transfer (ACT), a trading name of Farhat Trading Solutions Ltd. This pack separates driver-application terms from passenger booking terms. It does not replace a signed driver services agreement, certify legal compliance, or authorise publication, deployment, driver approval or deletion of records.

**Readiness:** the wording below is a proposed policy, not evidence that every described operational control is in use. Publication holds are identified in Part C. In particular, ACT's DBS-processing authority and supplier/international-transfer details must not be guessed. Obtain appropriate UK legal/privacy review for these points before publication.

## A. Driver Application Terms - proposed applicant-facing text

### 1. Purpose and contact
These terms explain how you apply to drive with ACT through its website. They cover application, verification and access to the application portal. Passenger bookings remain subject to ACT's separate passenger terms. Contact ACT about your application at **info@airportandcitytransfer.com** or **+44 20 8153 0303**.

Submitting an application does not guarantee approval, work, a particular number of journeys or earnings. Application terms do not set commission rates, deductions or pay arrangements. The intended self-employed basis and applicable tax responsibilities are described in section 1.1. Any terms governing actual driving work must be provided separately, without limiting rights that apply by law.

### 1.1 Self-employed basis and tax responsibilities
ACT's intended engagement model is exclusively self-employed: drivers apply to provide services as independent contractors, rather than for salaried employment.

Where a driver is self-employed for tax purposes, the driver is responsible for managing their own tax affairs. This includes registering with HMRC for Self Assessment where required; keeping accurate income, expense and supporting records; declaring all taxable earnings; submitting Self Assessment tax returns and any other required returns or digital updates by the applicable deadlines; and paying their own Income Tax and National Insurance contributions due. VAT registration, returns and payments, and Making Tax Digital obligations, are also the driver's responsibility where applicable.

ACT does not undertake to prepare or submit a driver's personal tax returns or pay the driver's personal tax liabilities on their behalf. Drivers should obtain independent accounting or tax advice where needed.

Nothing in these terms determines employment or tax status by label alone, excludes statutory rights, or removes any tax, PAYE, National Insurance, withholding or reporting obligation that the law places on ACT. Employment-rights status and tax status may differ; each is determined by the actual working arrangements and applicable law.

### 2. Accurate information and eligibility
Provide genuine, current information and readable copies of the documents requested. Apply in your own name and do not use another person's documents or account. ACT's application currently requires applicants to be at least 21. Contact ACT promptly when information changes or a licence, insurance policy or other required permission expires, is suspended, is revoked or becomes restricted.

Vehicle dropdowns and any future lookup results help complete the form; they do not establish a vehicle's licensing, insurance or suitability. You remain responsible for checking the details before submission. ACT independently reviews driver and vehicle eligibility before making a final decision.

### 3. One application and secure document submission
The application brings together personal details, TfL/driving information and vehicle evidence, followed by review and deliberate submission. You are not normally asked to repeat a separate standard document stage. ACT may request a particular missing, corrected or renewed document through the application portal.

Use the designated portal controls, not public links or ordinary email attachments, for identity and vetting documents. Documents may be PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 10 MB each; the driver photograph must be JPG or PNG. A file shown as selected has not necessarily been sent. Submission success and an application reference confirm receipt, not verification or approval.

### 4. DBS evidence and approval sequence
You may submit the initial application without a DBS certificate. If ACT accepts the application in principle and still needs the certificate, ACT will ask you to provide it through the secure application portal. ACT will explain the relevant check and request only information it is entitled and needs to process.

**Initial acceptance, an application reference, a password or access to the portal do not approve you to drive for ACT.** You cannot receive journeys until ACT has completed the required DBS, driver and vehicle checks and explicitly given final approval. ACT's review of your application does not replace TfL's licensing decisions.

### 5. Vehicle ownership, rental and lawful use
Choose the arrangement that accurately describes your use of the vehicle. An applicant using the owner route is asked for the V5C logbook and the other required vehicle evidence. An applicant renting, hiring or leasing a PHV is asked for the rental, hire or lease agreement showing their right to use it. For another lawful arrangement, explain it and provide the appropriate supporting evidence.

A rental agreement does not replace checks of the vehicle's PHV licence, hire-and-reward insurance, MOT and registered-keeper details. Do not describe a rented vehicle as owned simply to complete the form. ACT must verify a suitable licensed vehicle and relevant evidence before allocating journeys.

You may apply without a vehicle. If you later obtain one, use ACT's existing vehicle-supplement process rather than creating another account. Approval to receive journeys remains blocked until the vehicle checks are complete.

### 6. Password setup and account safety
ACT sends a time-limited password-setup link after successful submission. The current setup link is valid for 24 hours and is intended for one use. Keep the link and your password private. ACT will not ask you to send your password by email or messaging.

If a link expires or does not arrive, use the setup-link recovery function or contact ACT. Do not submit duplicate applications to obtain another link. A successfully set password provides access to the application portal while verification continues.

### 7. ACT review and further evidence
An authorised ACT reviewer makes the final approval decision. The reviewer may request targeted corrections, inspect documents and check relevant licences and permissions. ACT can hold or refuse an application where necessary evidence is absent, inconsistent, expired or not satisfactory. You may contact ACT to correct information or ask for a human explanation of the application decision, subject to lawful restrictions on disclosure.

Required operator-record information, including National Insurance and registered-keeper details where applicable, is collected through the restricted verification process by final approval. Do not send it in public comments, screenshots or ordinary support messages. No applicant may bypass outstanding checks because an email or portal screen was received successfully.

### 8. Passenger-facing information and privacy
If approved and assigned a journey, the necessary driver and vehicle identification details will be provided to the passenger, including your first name, PHV driver licence number, photograph and vehicle registration/details. Operational contact information may also be provided where needed for the journey. Original identity documents, DBS material and your National Insurance number are not passenger-facing information.

The Driver Application and Verification Privacy Notice explains processing and retention. Acknowledging that notice is not blanket consent to every use of your data, and is not permission to use your information for marketing.

### 9. Withdrawal, concerns and changes
You may withdraw an application or report an error by contacting ACT and identifying the relevant application without including unnecessary sensitive documents. Withdrawal does not automatically erase records that ACT must lawfully retain. ACT should tell you about material changes affecting your application; revised terms must not silently rewrite previously agreed passenger or driver contracts.

**Proposed review declaration:** I confirm that the information I have supplied is accurate, accept the Driver Application Terms, and acknowledge that I have read the Driver Application and Verification Privacy Notice. I understand that receipt of my application and password setup do not constitute final approval to receive journeys. I acknowledge the intended self-employed basis and my applicable tax responsibilities set out in section 1.1, without waiving any statutory rights.

## B. Driver Application and Verification Privacy Notice - proposed applicant-facing text

### 1. Who is responsible
Farhat Trading Solutions Ltd, trading as Airport & City Transfer (ACT), is responsible for the driver-application and verification processing described here. Contact **info@airportandcitytransfer.com**, marked 'Driver privacy request', or **+44 20 8153 0303**. This draft does not claim that ACT has appointed a statutory Data Protection Officer.

**Before publication:** confirm and insert the company's current correspondence address and any further verified controller details needed for the notice. Do not use an unverified historic address.

### 2. Information covered
The application uses your name, contact details, address/postcode, date of birth, driving experience, licence identifiers and expiry dates; vehicle description, registration, class, ownership or rental arrangement, insurance and MOT details; the required documents and photograph; application declarations, status and relevant correspondence. Account details and necessary security/audit information support access and administration.

The restricted final-review process also records National Insurance information, registered-keeper details and when a driver or vehicle becomes or ceases to be available to ACT. DBS-related information is handled only for the specific verification purpose and with the additional legal authority and safeguards described below. Do not send unrelated medical, financial or other sensitive information.

### 3. Where information comes from
Most information comes from you through the application, later requested corrections and contact with ACT. ACT also creates review outcomes and operator records, and checks relevant driver and vehicle licensing information with the appropriate sources. Where ACT obtains information about you from another source, it must explain that source and purpose unless a lawful exception applies. [S1]

The form requests Google Places address suggestions as you type sufficient address characters. You can enter an address manually, but that currently does not disable the suggestion requests. Uploaded identity documents are not part of those address-suggestion requests. Any future vehicle-registration lookup requires a suitable provider, a clear explanation and appropriate data safeguards; it is not represented here as already enabled.

### 4. Purposes and proposed lawful bases
ACT uses ordinary application information to assess your request to enter a working arrangement and administer that application: the proposed basis is steps at your request before a contract, Article 6(1)(b). Required driver and vehicle operator records are processed to meet the applicable legal obligations, Article 6(1)(c). Relevant obligations include the London operator-record rules and TfL licence requirements. [S2]

Necessary fraud prevention, account security and handling of application disputes may rely on legitimate interests, Article 6(1)(f), after ACT has documented necessity, proportionality and your rights. These interests are preventing misuse of the application system and making defensible, accurate application decisions. This draft does not substitute for that assessment. [S1]

**DBS publication hold:** ACT must confirm and identify the specific UK legal authority/condition permitting its processing of criminal-offence information, in addition to the ordinary lawful basis. Where applicable, the assessment must address Article 10, a relevant Data Protection Act 2018 Schedule 1 condition, an appropriate policy document and a data protection impact assessment. A general consent checkbox, contract or legitimate-interest statement alone does not establish this additional authority. Do not replace this paragraph with an invented condition. [S3, S4]

Required information is identified in the form or by the reviewer. Without information necessary to complete lawful checks, ACT may be unable to progress or approve the application. Optional DBS upload at initial submission does not waive required checks before final approval.

### 5. Who may receive information
Access is limited to authorised ACT staff with an application, compliance or operational need, and technical suppliers providing the relevant hosting, storage, security or communications service. Regulators, police, courts or professional advisers may receive necessary information where legally required or otherwise lawfully justified. Driver/vehicle identification needed for an assigned journey is shared with the relevant passenger; original application evidence and NI/DBS information are not included in that passenger disclosure.

**Supplier and transfer publication hold:** complete the actual recipient/processor inventory, data-location and overseas-access review before publication. Describe any restricted international transfers and the applicable safeguards, with a way to obtain further information. The fact that ACT operates in the UK does not establish that all suppliers process data only in the UK. Do not assert adequacy, an IDTA or contractual safeguards without verifying the relevant arrangement. [S1, S5]

### 6. How long information is kept
The proposed notice carries forward ACT's owner-approved retention arrangement, rather than applying one period to every document:

- **Required driver and vehicle operator records:** while the driver or vehicle is available to ACT, and for 12 months after the relevant cessation of availability, subject to a documented lawful hold. [S2]
- **Necessary unsuccessful-application records:** normally six months after rejection or withdrawal, or after the last activity for an abandoned application. This is ACT's chosen business policy, not a universal statutory six-month rule.
- **Unnecessary original DBS/vetting material:** remove as soon as reasonably practicable when its verification purpose is complete; retain only a justified check outcome where appropriate. A certificate is not automatically kept for six or twelve months merely because another application record has that period. [S4]
- **Release recovery archives:** review 30 days after the relevant release for continued necessity and secure disposal or further justified retention. This is a review point, not a promise that every backup is automatically erased on day 30.
- **Legal, regulatory or complaint holds:** restrict continued retention to what is needed, document the reason and review the hold. Records are not retained indefinitely merely because a possible future purpose can be imagined. [S6]

The agreed arrangement uses monthly manual retention review, not automatic deletion. Actual disposal remains a controlled operation. Backup lifecycle and treatment of restored data must be checked so that an older recovery copy does not silently undo completed deletion decisions. Do not promise instant removal from every recovery copy. [S6]

### 7. Security and decisions
Application evidence is intended for controlled-access private storage and authorised review, rather than public media links. Restricted operator information is handled separately from passenger-facing information. ACT must verify the actual safeguards described in the published notice; this draft does not claim that every system is encrypted or every supplier audited.

Form validation checks missing or invalid information. It is not final driver approval. An authorised ACT reviewer makes the eligibility decision in this workflow. The proposed notice does not authorise AI-based final approval, the use of original documents for AI training, or new automation that has not been separately reviewed.

### 8. Your choices, rights and complaints
You may ask to access or correct your information. Depending on the circumstances, you may also request erasure, restriction or portability, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. These rights are subject to applicable limits, including records ACT is legally required to keep. Where a specific use genuinely relies on consent, you can withdraw that consent without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing. [S1]

Send requests or privacy concerns to ACT using the contact above. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at **ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/**. You do not need to agree that ACT's handling was correct merely because you acknowledged this notice. Acknowledgement of this notice is separate from any optional marketing choice.

### 9. Version and scope
This notice concerns driver applications, verification and the operator-record information described above. Separate passenger-booking and any later driving-service privacy information must remain consistent with it. ACT should explain material new purposes before introducing them. The final published version must have an effective date and remain available from the application form. [S1]
